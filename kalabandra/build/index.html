— Viking Band from Stockholm, Sweden —

KALABALIK is a viking band from Stockholm, Sweden, which play original contemporary medieval music on a wide range of historic instruments.

The band has toured the nordic countries since 2009, making appearances on a host of medieval and viking themed markets, weddings and parties.
